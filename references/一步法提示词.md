# 一步法提示词（主流程）

模型：GPT Image 2.5（apimart 里叫 image2.5；Codex 自带生图也能用）· 3:4 · 2K 左右。不同工具怎么传参数见 SKILL.md 第五节。
没有比例参数的工具（如 Codex 自带生图）：在模板第一句前面加「A vertical 3:4 photo.」。
参考图顺序：① 人物资产 ② 姿势照 ③ 脸部特写（客户实拍、全身照脸小时才加）。场景不给图，只写字。
模板按女性写；资产是男性时把 woman / she / her 全部换掉。
模板里的「white clothes / white camisole」是按样本4 的白吊带写的，换成这次资产上最浅的那件衣服（white T-shirt、beige coat…）；没有浅色衣服就写「her clothes take the same color cast as the room」。

## 模板（英文写，大括号里换成这次的内容）

```
A candid documentary photograph taken inside {具体地点，带年代/状态，如 a real, slightly worn 1990s Hong Kong cha chaan teng during the afternoon}: {两三样看得见的东西，如 red vinyl booth seats, formica tables with metal edges, green-and-white mosaic floor tiles, handwritten menu boards on off-white tiled walls}, {两三处生活痕迹，如 a few chipped tiles, a stack of plastic stools and a mop bucket in the corner, scuffs on the floor}, {场景里的光源，如 cool-white fluorescent tubes on the ceiling}. A young woman {她在场景里的位置和动作，如 stands in the aisle beside a table}.
Her pose is copied from image 2 (ignore that person's clothes, face, background and studio light): {姿势，全部用画面左/右写}. Same left/right orientation as image 2, do not mirror. She holds this pose naturally, not stiff.
Image 1 is ONLY an identity reference for who she is: {脸部特征、发型、身材、衣服}. Do NOT copy image 1's studio lighting, grey backdrop, skin retouching or pose. She wears {鞋}.
{有第 3 张脸部特写时，把上一句换成：Images 1 and 3 are ONLY identity references for who she is. Her face must be exactly the woman in image 3 (a close-up of her face): {五官}; she is the same person, not a look-alike. Image 1 shows her build and exactly the outfit she wears: {衣服，写出材质}. Do NOT copy the studio backdrop, studio lighting, skin retouching or pose of images 1 and 3.}
She is photographed in this place by the same light as everything else: only {场景光源和色温}; {光从哪来、哪里有影子}; her white clothes are the same off-white as {场景里的白色物体}; she is not brighter than {背景}; natural unretouched skin with visible texture; the same fine film grain, slight softness and color cast on her as on the room; soft shadows of her shoes on the {地面}.
Camera: a real, unretouched snapshot taken by a friend with a 50mm lens on a mirrorless camera, about {3～4} meters away, held at {waist height / 机位}, level; straight-out-of-camera JPEG with no retouching and no color grading: natural, slightly muted colors; real dynamic range, so {场景里最亮的东西，如 the ceiling tubes / the sky} clip to white and the deepest shadows fall almost to black, with a little noise in the shadows; framed a little loosely and slightly off-center as if taken quickly; natural undistorted proportions; the far background slightly out of focus. Not a fashion editorial, not a cinematic film still: no teal-and-orange grading, no HDR look, no beauty lighting. Real scale: {用场景里的物件定尺寸，如 the table top is at her hip, the booth backrest at her waist}. {公共场所写：Far in the background two or three ordinary people, small and out of focus, none of them near her; she is the only person in focus. 私人空间写：Only one person.}
```

## 真实感那几句（10/22 对照测试，3/3 明显更像真照片）
同一个人、同一个姿势、同一个场景，只加这几句重出：便利店 #6990 → #7006，旺角夜街 #6987 → #7008，小樽雪街 #6991 → #7010。
- **生活痕迹**：货架空位、价签、拖把桶、垃圾袋、贴满小广告的卷帘门、踩过的雪和车辙。不写的话场景像刚布置好的样板间。
- **公共场所放远处路人**：原来写「Only one person」，旺角深夜、便利店、雪街空无一人，像布景。改成「远处两三个虚化的路人，离她都很远；对焦清楚的只有她」，3 张都没有人跑到她旁边。家里、天台这类私人空间仍写 Only one person。
- **相机直出、不调色、真实动态范围**：去掉了青红「赛博」调色和 HDR 感，亮灯和天空会过曝，暗部有噪点。
- **衣服带点使用痕迹**（a little creased, damp at the hem）：风衣不再像刚从衣架上拿下来。
- 没起作用的：「framed slightly off-center」3 张里 2 张还是正中间。要偏构图，出图后自己裁（交图前告诉用户）。

## 光那一段：按场景换写法（模板里是室内日光灯的版本）

- **室内日光灯**：only the overhead fluorescent tubes, slightly cool and greenish; top-down light with soft shadows in her eye sockets, under her nose and chin; her white camisole is the same off-white as the wall tiles; she is not brighter than the wall.
- **夜景霓虹 / 街灯**：only the {颜色} neon from the signs and the {店铺/路灯} spill; the light falls on her from {方向}, the side of her face away from the signs in shadow; her face and clothes pick up the {颜色} of the signs; her white clothes take the same tint as {场景里的白色物件，如 the white sticker paper on the shutter}; she is not brighter than the lit signs behind her; a soft dark contact shadow and a faint wet reflection under her shoes.
- **户外白天**：only the {晴天/阴天} daylight from {方向}; {硬/柔}shadows matching the shadows of {场景里的物件}; her whites match the whites in the street; same haze and contrast as the background.
- **室内窗光 + 暖光**：cool daylight from the window at the frame's {左/右} and the warm glow of {灯/炉} at the frame's {右/左}; the window side of her face lit, the other side in soft shadow.

## 镜头和尺寸：按姿势换

| 姿势 | 机位 | 用物件定尺寸（例） |
|---|---|---|
| 站 | 腰高，3～4 米 | the table top is at her hip, the booth backrest at her waist |
| 蹲 | 胸口以下（约 70 厘米），3 米 | squatting, her head is only a little above the top of the stacked stools; the shutter rises far above her |
| 坐凳子 | 胸口以下，3 米 | the bench seat is at about her knee height; the skis behind her are taller than her seated head |
| 坐地上 | 胸口以下，3 米 | seated on the floor, her head is a little below the booth backrest beside her |
| 跪立 | 约 80 厘米，3 米 | kneeling upright, her bowed head is at about the top of the lowest dryer |
| 趴 / 躺 | 贴地（约 20～30 厘米），离脸 2.5 米 | her raised feet reach about the height of the sofa seat behind her |

横着躺、身体沿画面左右展开的姿势（仰躺、侧躺）：3:4 竖图里人只占下面一条，上面大半是背景（10/22 #6989）；改 4:3 后人占满画面宽（10/22 #7028 沙滩侧躺）。报价时问用户要不要改 4:3；保持 3:4 就写 her body fills the width of the frame, the camera close enough that she takes up the lower half of the frame。

场景里有镜子（理发店、试衣间、健身房、电梯）时加一句：The wall mirror is at the side of the frame and angled away, so it reflects only the empty room, not her. 不写的话镜子里该有她，「Only one person」会和镜子打架（10/21 理发店加了这句，镜子里没有多出人）。

## 每段为什么这么写

- **场景在最前、人「在场景里」**：先有地方，人是被拍进去的。反过来写成「把人放进场景」，模型就会做贴图。
- **两三样看得见的东西**：只写地名会出明信片式的空泛画面；写出具体物件，场景才像真去过。
- **姿势写画面左右 + 不要镜像**：写「她的左手」最容易镜像。看参考图时从画面角度描述：哪只手在画面左边、在干什么。
- **资产只用来认人**：不写这句，资产图的影棚光、磨皮、灰背景、站姿都会被带进成片，人又像棚拍的（10/20 #6928 就是这样）。
- **鞋**：资产常是光脚，光脚出现在餐厅、街头一看就假；在家里趴着、躺着时光脚反而自然（#6984）。
- **「She holds this pose naturally, not stiff」**：原来写的「Natural and relaxed, not a stiff studio pose」会让模型把摆出来的时装姿势放松走样（10/21 理发店 #6973）。
- **第 3 张脸部特写**：全身照里脸太小，模型只学到发型和眉毛，脸会换成另一个人（#6983、#6986）；加特写后锁住（#6987）。
- **光只写场景光，并写「人不比墙亮、白衣服和墙同色」**：不写的话人会是全画面最亮最干净的东西，像被单独打了光（10/20 #6934 加上这句后统一了）。
- **镜头 50mm、腰高、3～4 米**：不写镜头时模型常用广角近拍，腿被拉长、头变小。
- **用物件定尺寸**：桌面到胯、卡座靠背到腰，这类参照能把人的大小管住。
- **地点写具体但别要求具体店名招牌字**：可以写真实街区（旺角、白马），但不要让模型写出特定的店招文字，生成的中文招牌常乱码；主体附近别放大字招牌。
- **身份特征**：痣、雀斑按「她脸上的位置」写（on her left cheek near the corner of her mouth）即可；画面左右只用于姿势。资产是正面照时要换算：画面左边的痣在她的右脸。

## 姿势描述怎么写（例子）

- 站：standing facing the camera with the weight on one leg, feet slightly apart; the arm on the frame's left bent up with the fingertips touching her chin; the arm on the frame's right hanging down with the hand tucked into her jeans pocket; head slightly tilted.
- 蹲：a deep squat on her heels in side view, body facing the frame's left, knees pulled up close to her chest, both forearms resting over her knees with the hands loosely clasped in front of the knees at the frame's left, head turned toward the camera.
- 坐：sitting on the floor facing the camera, knees bent and close together in front of her, lower legs folded out to both sides with the feet near her hips at the frame's left and right, both hands resting together on the floor between her knees, back upright, looking at the camera.
- 侧坐后撑：sitting on the ground in side view, her body and legs facing the frame's left; both knees bent up high and close together, feet flat on the ground at the frame's left; the arm on the frame's right reaches straight down behind her hip with the palm flat on the ground at the frame's right; her other hand flat on the ground just behind her hips, mostly hidden; head turned back over her shoulder, looking into the lens.
- 趴：lying on her stomach, head and shoulders at the frame's left close to the camera, body stretching away toward the frame's right; one forearm flat on the floor toward the frame's left; the other elbow on the floor with that hand pushed into the hair on top of her head; lower legs bent up behind her, crossed at the ankles, soles pointing up.
- 跪立低头：kneeling upright in side view facing the frame's right; thighs vertical, lower legs flat on the floor behind her toward the frame's left; both arms hanging straight down; head bowed far forward so her long hair hides her face.
- 蹲下伸手：a deep squat on the balls of her feet, body turned three-quarters toward the frame's left; the arm on the frame's left stretched straight out and down, the relaxed hand low near the ground (reaching toward a cat); the other hand tucking her hair behind her ear; looking into the lens.
- 侧躺枕臂：lying on her side facing the camera, head at the frame's left; the arm underneath lies past her head toward the frame's left with its forearm bent up, hand resting loosely above her head; her head rests on that upper arm; the other arm comes down in front of her chest, hand tucked under her cheek.
- 跪坐分腿（W 坐）：knees close together in front of her, lower legs folded out to both sides with the feet near her hips at the frame's left and right; both hands resting together between her knees.
- 坐地交叉腿头靠膝：sitting on the ground facing the camera; the leg at the frame's right bent up high, she rests the side of her head on top of that knee; her other leg lies folded across in front: the thigh points toward the frame's right, the knee bent, the shin crosses back in front of her raised foot so this foot ends far out at the frame's left; one hand flat on the ground beside her hip, the other around the outside of the raised knee, flat on the ground at the frame's right.
- 侧转站（一步法容易拉正，要写到肩和胯）：her chest turned about 45 degrees toward the frame's left, the shoulder on the frame's right closer to the camera; weight on the leg at the frame's left so that hip juts out in an S-curve; the elbow on the frame's right points back and out, leaving a triangle of background between arm and waist.
